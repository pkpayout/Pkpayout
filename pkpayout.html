FAQ

Common Questions
Answered.

How fast are settlements processed? +
Most corridors settle in under 2 seconds. Pakistan domestic (JazzCash, EasyPaisa) and India (UPI) are typically under 1 second. All other corridors complete within minutes, never hours.
Which payment methods do you support in Pakistan? +
We support JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Raast, HBL, UBL, Meezan Bank, and all major Pakistani commercial banks via direct bank transfer. New methods are added regularly based on partner needs.
Is there a minimum transaction volume required? +
We work with a range of operators — from individuals to enterprises. Contact us on Telegram (@pkpayout_bot) to discuss your volume and get a custom rate for your corridor.
How do I get started or become a partner? +
Simply reach us on Telegram via @pkpayout_bot. We'll discuss your requirements, target corridors, and onboard you within 24 hours. No lengthy forms — just a direct conversation.
How is counterparty risk managed? +
We operate a pre-funded settlement model. Liquidity is reserved before transactions are processed, eliminating the dependency and risk associated with traditional P2P arrangements. Your funds are guaranteed, not contingent.
Do you provide transaction reporting? +
Yes. All partners receive real-time transaction confirmations via Telegram. Full reporting including settlement history, corridor performance, and balance statements is available on request.
